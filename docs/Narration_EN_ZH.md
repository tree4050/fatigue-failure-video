Why can a metal part fail even when no single load is large enough to break it?

**00:00:07.367–00:00:14.450**

Fatigue is damage from repeated loading. It can occur when nominal stress stays below yield strength.

疲勞是反覆載重造成的損傷。即使名義應力低於材料的降伏強度，也可能發生破壞。

**00:00:38.565–00:00:42.533**

A scratch concentrates stress locally, and a small crack begins.

刮痕造成局部應力集中，微小裂紋因而起始。

**00:00:43.351–00:00:45.612**

Repeated tension opens the crack.

反覆拉伸使裂紋張開。

**00:00:46.430–00:00:51.998**

As cycle count N increases, crack length a grows, leaving less intact material.

隨著循環次數 N 增加，裂紋長度 a 增長，剩餘完整的材料逐漸減少。

**00:00:52.816–00:00:58.128**

The gradual growth may be hard to see. The sudden break is only the final stage.

逐漸發展的裂紋可能很難察覺。突然斷裂只是整個過程的最後階段。

**00:00:58.946–00:01:04.450**

Once the crack reaches a critical condition, the remaining section fractures rapidly.

當裂紋達到臨界狀態，剩餘截面便快速斷裂。

**00:01:05.550–00:01:09.155**

An S–N curve connects stress amplitude to fatigue life.

S–N 曲線把應力振幅與疲勞壽命連結起來。

**00:01:09.428–00:01:13.119**

S is half the difference between maximum and minimum stress.

S 是最大應力與最小應力之差的一半。

**00:01:13.392–00:01:17.104**

The vertical axis is stress amplitude in megapascals.

縱軸是應力振幅，單位為百萬帕斯卡，也就是 MPa。

**00:01:17.378–00:01:22.199**

The horizontal axis shows cycles to failure on a logarithmic scale.

橫軸用對數刻度表示試件破壞前所承受的循環次數。

**00:01:22.472–00:01:26.525**

Choose a stress, move across to the curve, then down to read life.

選定應力振幅，水平移到曲線，再垂直向下讀出疲勞壽命。

**00:01:26.798–00:01:32.004**

For the same material and conditions, lower amplitude usually means longer life.

對相同材料和試驗條件而言，較低的應力振幅通常代表較長的壽命。

**00:01:32.277–00:01:33.856**

Actual results scatter.

實際試驗結果具有分散性。

**00:01:34.129–00:01:38.182**

Some steels show a fatigue limit under specific test conditions.

某些鋼材在特定試驗條件下會呈現疲勞限。

**00:01:38.455–00:01:44.450**

Aluminum alloys generally do not. These limits are not universal safety guarantees.

鋁合金通常沒有明確的疲勞限。這些試驗中的極限，也不是適用於所有情況的安全保證。

**00:01:45.550–00:01:49.326**

Fracture toughness measures resistance to fracture with a crack.

斷裂韌性衡量材料已經存在裂紋時，抵抗斷裂的能力。

**00:01:49.564–00:01:54.386**

K I describes crack tip loading: the Mode one stress intensity factor.

K I 描述裂紋尖端的載重狀態，是第一型，也就是張開型的應力強度因子。

**00:01:54.624–00:01:59.702**

Y describes geometry; sigma is tensile stress; a measures edge to tip.

Y 反映幾何形狀；sigma 是施加的拉伸應力；a 是從試件邊緣到裂紋尖端的長度。

**00:01:59.940–00:02:03.460**

Raising stress or lengthening the crack increases K I.

提高應力，或使裂紋增長，都會增加 K I。

**00:02:03.698–00:02:08.626**

K I c is material plane strain fracture toughness, not yield strength.

K I c 是材料的平面應變斷裂韌性，並不是降伏強度。

**00:02:08.865–00:02:16.716**

Under linear elastic, valid plane strain conditions, peak K I reaching K I c can trigger rapid fracture.

在線彈性且符合有效平面應變條件下，最大 K I 達到 K I c 時，可能觸發快速斷裂。

**00:02:16.954–00:02:19.450**

Fatigue cracks can grow below this limit.

疲勞裂紋在低於這個臨界值時，也能逐漸成長。

**00:02:20.550–00:02:23.387**

Repeated loading can cause fatigue damage.

反覆載重可能造成疲勞損傷。

**00:02:25.452–00:02:29.078**

S–N curves relate stress amplitude to fatigue life.

S–N 曲線描述應力振幅與疲勞壽命的關係。

**00:02:31.143–00:02:35.452**

Fracture toughness helps assess when an existing crack becomes critical.

斷裂韌性有助於評估既有裂紋何時達到臨界狀態。

**00:02:37.517–00:02:44.450**

Reduce stress concentrations, improve surface condition, and inspect for cracks to reduce fatigue risk.

降低應力集中、改善表面狀況，並檢查裂紋，可以降低疲勞破壞的風險。
